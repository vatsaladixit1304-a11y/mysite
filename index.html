The Girl in the Window

Some secrets should never be discovered...

It was a cold and rainy night when Riya returned to her grandmother's old house. The house was far away from the city and was surrounded by tall trees and an empty road. Her grandmother had passed away a few days ago and Riya had come to collect her belongings.

The house looked exactly as she remembered it, except for one thing. The window on the second floor was open. Her grandmother had always kept that window locked and warned Riya to never go near it.

That night when Riya was sitting in her room when she heard three knocks on the wall. Knock... Knock... Knock. She ignored the voice thinking it was just the wind. But then she heard a girl's voice whisper, "Riya, please let me in."

Riya froze as she was alone in the house. She slowly opened her bedroom door and looked into the hallway, nobody was there. Just as she was about to close the door, she noticed a wet footprint on the floor. Then another. And another.

The footprints were coming from the stairs and leading straight to her bedroom. But there were no footprints leading away. Someone or something was already inside.

Suddenly, her phone buzzed. It was a message from an unknown number. "Don't look at the window." Riya stared at the screen, confused. Another message arrived. "She knows you're here."

Unable to control her curiosity, Riya slowly turned towards the window. A little girl stood outside, even though her room was on the second floor. Her long black hair covered her face, and her white dress was dripping wet.

The girl raised her head and smiled. Then she lifted one hand and pointed behind Riya. Riya quickly turned around but the room was empty. When she looked back at the window the girl had disappeared.

Riya was terrified she ran downstairs and found an old photograph lying on a table. It showed her grandmother standing beside a little girl. On the back, something was written "Meera, missing since 1987."

Riya remembered a story her grandmother had told her when she was little. A girl named Meera had disappeared from the house many years ago. People said she was last seen near the upstairs window. Her grandmother had never spoken about her again.

Suddenly, the lights went out. From upstairs came the sound of a door creaking. Then a little girl's voice called, "Riya, I found you!"

Riya ran towards the front door, but she was unable to open it. She pulled the handle again and again. Then her phone rang. This time the caller ID showed her grandmother's name.

With trembling hands, she answered. Her grandmother's voice whispered, "Listen carefully. Whatever happens, do not let Meera leave the room."

Riya's heart stopped. "But Grandma, she's already outside!" There was silence on the phone. Then her grandmother replied, "No, darling. Meera has been dead for years. The thing outside your window is not Meera."

A loud knock came from the front door. Riya turned around. Someone was standing on the other side speaking in her voice.

"Riya, open the door. It's me. Please let me in."

Her phone slipped from her hand. Slowly, she looked at the dark window beside her. Her reflection was standing there, smiling. But Riya wasn't smiling.

The next morning, the neighbours found the house empty. There was no sign of Riya anywhere. Everything looked normal, except for one thing. The upstairs window was finally closed.

And every night, at exactly midnight, a new girl could be seen standing behind it.
